### Important Checklist for your GitHub Repository:

1.  **`requirements.txt`**: Create a file named `requirements.txt` and paste this list into it so others can easily set up the environment:
    ```text
    pandas
    numpy
    scikit-learn
    imblearn
    joblib
    gradio
    matplotlib
    seaborn
    ```
2.  **Screenshots**: Don't forget to take a screenshot of your **Gradio Dashboard** and your **Feature Importance graph**. Save them in a folder called `/images` and link them in the README using `![Dashboard](images/your_screenshot.png)`. This is the single most effective way to grab a recruiter's attention.

3.  **Project Structure**: Keep your folder clean:
    * `/notebooks`: Your `.ipynb` file.
    * `/data`: Your `Finance_Trends.csv`.
    * `/models`: Your `.pkl` files.
    * `/images`: Your screenshots.
    * `README.md`: The file above.
    * `requirements.txt`: The dependencies.
